Try the prototype

Touchscreen: press and hold a row, drag up or down, then release.
Desktop: click a row, then Shift-click another.

Hold to start

Press a row for about a third of a second. A regular tap still toggles just one item.

Drag to extend

Keep your finger down and move over the list. A preview marks the range; release to add it.

Scroll through long lists

Keep dragging near the top or bottom edge to scroll the page and extend the range across groups.

Track what is selected

The selected total, group progress, and row counts stay visible. Select a whole group or the full list when that is quicker.

Keyboard controls

↑ / ↓ move; Home / End jump. Space or Enter toggles; hold Shift for a range. Ctrl / ⌘ + A selects all; Esc clears.
